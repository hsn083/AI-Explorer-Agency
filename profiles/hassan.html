About Hassan

Hassan is a full-stack developer specializing in the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js) who combines technical expertise with a user-centered approach to web development. With experience across multiple MERN stack projects and modern web technologies, he brings both technical depth and practical problem-solving skills to every challenge. Hassan is committed to staying current with emerging JavaScript frameworks while maintaining focus on proven best practices that ensure reliability and performance.
