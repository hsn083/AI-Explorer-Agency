About Amna

Amna specializes in digital marketing and search engine optimization, helping businesses build meaningful online presence that actually drives results. Her approach combines data-driven insights with creative marketing strategies to connect brands with their target audiences effectively.

With deep expertise in technical SEO, on-page optimization, keyword research, and performance marketing, Amna develops comprehensive strategies that improve search visibility while building authentic engagement with potential customers. Role at AI Explorer

Amna leads digital marketing and SEO initiatives for both AI Explorer and its clients. She develops comprehensive marketing strategies that align with business objectives, ensuring that digital efforts contribute directly to growth and success metrics. Her expertise covers the full spectrum of search engine optimization, from technical audits to content strategy implementation.

Her role involves conducting in-depth keyword research, performing technical SEO audits, analyzing competitive landscapes, and identifying opportunities for improved search visibility and user engagement. Amna works closely with the development team to ensure technical SEO best practices, proper site architecture, and optimal page speed are implemented across all client projects.

She also provides ongoing performance analysis using Google Analytics and Search Console, delivering detailed reports on keyword rankings, organic traffic growth, conversion rates, and ROI. Amna helps clients understand the impact of their digital investments and make data-driven decisions about future marketing efforts, campaign scaling, and budget allocation.
